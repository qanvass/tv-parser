# Search & SEO Keywords Map

This document lists the primary search indexing phrases and keyword mappings relevant to the TV Parser repository. Use these tags and natural phrases in blog posts, store listings, and repository descriptions.

---

## 🎯 Target Search Terms

### 1. Flutter IPTV Player Shells
* **Keywords:** `flutter-iptv`, `iptv-player-flutter`, `m3u-player-app-shell`
* **Intent:** Developers looking for a structured, clean-coded player application codebase in Dart/Flutter.
* **Context:** Highlight the BLoC state architecture, local SQL/caching, and separation of app UI from media content.

### 2. Android TV Media Players
* **Keywords:** `android-tv-player`, `fire-tv-app`, `10-foot-ui-flutter`
* **Intent:** TV developers or researchers looking to customize a player interface for smart TV sticks and set-top boxes.
* **Context:** Emphasize native Android TV D-pad focus handlers, scaling animations, and TV navigation rail setups.

### 3. Xtream Codes portals
* **Keywords:** `xtream-codes-api`, `xtream-player-shell`, `portal-login-player`
* **Intent:** Users searching for a media player shell compatible with standardized Xtream portal login endpoints.
* **Context:** Emphasize the compliance-safe player structure (users enter their own URL, username, and password).

---

## 🔒 SEO & Compliance Policy

- **No Copyright Violations:** Never associate search descriptions with phrases like "Free Channels", "Unblocked TV", or "Free Streaming Subscriptions".
- **Focus on Player Utility:** Position the app as a legal media player client, comparable to VLC, Kodi, or MX Player.
